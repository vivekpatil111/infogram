# Infogram: The Strategic Transformation Blueprint

## 1. The Fundamental Shift
**Instagram** is a "Mirror" (reflecting a curated life).
**Infogram** is a "Lens" (focusing on a specific piece of knowledge).

## 2. Product Feature Map (Clone $\rightarrow$ Pivot)

### A. The Feed $\rightarrow$ The Knowledge Stream
- **Clone**: Vertical scroll of high-quality videos.
- **Pivot**: Instead of "Popularity," the feed is sorted by **"Utility Score"**.
- **Utility Score** = $\frac{(Saves + Bookmarks)}{(Views)} \times \text{Expert Verification Weight}$.

### B. The Profile $\rightarrow$ The Knowledge Portfolio
- **Clone**: Grid of photos and reels.
- **Pivot**: A structured "Syllabus" of the creator's knowledge.
- **New Feature**: "Learning Paths" (e.g., "Mastering Startup Law" $\rightarrow$ a sequence of 10 reels).

### C. The Interaction $\rightarrow$ The Resource Drawer
- **Clone**: Like, Comment, Share.
- **Pivot**: The **Resource Drawer** (The "Aha!" Button).
    - **One-Tap Resources**: PDF guides, official website links, and checklists.
    - **Knowledge Tags**: Instead of #food, use #HowToRegisterCompany.

## 3. Monetization for Knowledge Creators
We move away from generic "Ad-roll" and toward "Value-based" earnings:
- **Tipping for Utility**: Users can "tip" a creator when a specific reel actually solves their problem.
- **Sponsored Education**: Companies sponsor "Knowledge Verticals" (e.g., a Law firm sponsoring the #LegalTips vertical).

## 4. Implementation Roadmap
1. **Phase 1 (The Shell)**: Build the Instagram-style UI (Virtual List, HLS Player).
2. **Phase 2 (The Brain)**: Implement the "Utility-based" ranking and Knowledge Verticals.
3. **Phase 3 (The Utility)**: Build the "Resource Drawer" and "Learning Paths".
4. **Phase 4 (The Ecosystem)**: Launch the Expert Verification and Value-based earnings.

---
**Core Mantra**: "Stop the Scroll, Start the Learning."
